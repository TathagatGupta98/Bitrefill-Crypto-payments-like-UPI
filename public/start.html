Set up your shop in one chat

All you need is Telegram. Printing the standee is optional, since your QR also works from a phone screen.

  1. Open Sikku on Telegram

    Tap the button, or scan the code from another device, then press Start.

  2. Answer three short questions

    Your shop's name, your phone number and how you'd like to be paid.

  3. Get your shop kit

    Sikku sends your payment QR, a standee to print and your soundbox link.

  4. Put it on your counter

    Place the QR where customers can scan it. Open the soundbox on a phone nearby, tap Start, and keep the volume up.

  5. Try it once yourself

    Open your own pay page and walk through it, so you know what customers will see.
