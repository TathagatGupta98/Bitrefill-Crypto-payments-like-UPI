Three steps, and you're open for crypto

No new app, no seed phrase and no crypto knowledge. If you can use Telegram, you can use Sikka.

1

Chat with Sikku

Answer three short questions on Telegram: your shop's name, your number and how you'd like to be paid.

2

Put up your QR

Print your standee and keep it next to your PhonePe QR. Open the soundbox on a phone at the counter.

3

Get paid

When a customer pays, you hear it, and a PhonePe gift card for that amount arrives in your chat.

Simple on both sides of the counter

It feels like UPI, because that's what people already know.

For shop owners

  • No crypto wallet or exchange account
  • A soundbox that speaks Hindi
  • Ask “aaj kitna aaya?” in plain Hinglish
  • Change your limits just by asking

For customers

  • Scan with the phone camera, no app to install
  • Pay with USDC on Base from any wallet
  • Clear live status, from payment to done
  • A receipt with a reference and on-chain proof

An agent with limits you control

Sikka buys without a human at checkout, so its limits live in code, not in an AI's judgement.

You set the limits

By default, anything over ₹500 needs your approval and sales stop at ₹2,000 a day.

You approve big sales

Over your limit, you get Approve and Reject buttons. The customer can't pay until you tap one.

AI can't touch money

The assistant only answers your questions and changes your limits within fixed ceilings. Every money step is plain code.

Good to know

Do I receive rupees in my bank account?

No. You receive a PhonePe gift card for the sale amount, which you add to PhonePe as gift card balance.

Who holds the customer's money?

For real purchases, the customer's USDC goes straight to the gift card provider, Bitrefill, to pay for your card. Sikka doesn't hold it.

What if a purchase fails?

The customer sees a clear screen with a reference and you get an alert on Telegram. Refunds are handled by hand for now.

Is this ready for my shop today?

Sikka is an early prototype built for a hackathon. Please treat it as a demo for now, not as a finished payment service.
