From scan to gift card, step by step

Here's everything that happens in one ₹100 sale, and who does what.

  1. Customer

    Scans your QR and picks ₹100

    The phone camera opens your shop's pay page. There's no app to install or account to create.

  2. Sikka

    Checks your limits first

    Before showing any payment details, Sikka checks your per-sale and daily limits. Over your per-sale limit, it asks you to approve in Telegram.

  3. Sikka + Bitrefill

    Creates an invoice for your card

    Bitrefill sets the exact USDC amount and a payment address just for this sale. The customer sees both, with a 15-minute timer.

  4. Customer

    Pays in USDC on Base

    They send the exact amount from their own wallet. Network confirmation can take a few minutes, and the page updates by itself.

  5. Sikka

    Delivers your gift card

    Once Bitrefill delivers, the customer's page turns green, your soundbox announces the sale, and the card arrives in your Telegram chat.

  6. You

    Add the card to PhonePe

    Claim it in PhonePe, then tap “Claimed” in the chat. Sikka then deletes its copy of the code.
